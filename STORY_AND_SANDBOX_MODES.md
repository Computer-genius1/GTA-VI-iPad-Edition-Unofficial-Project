# Story Mode and Sandbox Mode

This update adds a selectable game mode flow to the prototype.

## Story Mode — introductory mission chain
1. **Coastal Welcome** — reach the beachfront promenade.
2. **Warehouse Run** — travel to the warehouse district.
3. **Park Recon** — visit the neighborhood park to finish the introductory sequence.

Objectives display in the HUD and use a visible destination marker. The objective can be completed on foot or while driving. Mission progression is sequential.

## Sandbox Mode
Free roam with no mission progression or objective marker. Explore the procedural city, walk, and use the prototype vehicle.

## Current limitations
- The missions are simple destination/checkpoint objectives; no dialogue, cutscenes, combat, mission branching, or save/load system yet.
- These are original prototype missions and locations, not Rockstar/GTA VI missions.
- Menu and gameplay implementation have not yet been run in Godot or tested on an iPad.
