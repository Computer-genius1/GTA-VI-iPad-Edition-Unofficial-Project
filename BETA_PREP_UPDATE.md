# Beta-preparation update

## New prototype systems
- Ambient traffic cars with looping route points.
- Pedestrian placeholders strolling around the promenade.
- Crosswalk stripes and street lamp placements at selected intersections.
- Waterfront planter props to make the promenade less empty.
- Keyboard visual toggles: **N** for night ambience and **V** for cloudy ambience.

## Still experimental / not yet validated
- Traffic is placeholder geometry and has no road-aware collision, signals, or pathfinding.
- Pedestrians are simple primitive meshes without animation or navigation mesh.
- Night/weather switches are basic ambience changes, not full dynamic time or rain effects.
- Project still requires import, runtime, mobile performance, and iOS export testing in Godot/Xcode.
